Hi, nice to meet you!

I am Ruoxi Yang, currently a Senior Research Technician at The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center. I earned my M.S. in Biostatistics from New York University and my B.A. in Statistics from Rutgers University–New Brunswick.

My research interests include biomedical AI, bioinformatics, and statistical genomics.

🔥 News

  • Oct 2026: My first-author paper, Debiasing AI Evaluators for Radiology Report Generation was accepted for an oral presentation at SPIE Medical Imaging 2027 in Vancouver, Canada.
  • Jun 2026: My first-author paper, Prevalence Aware Feature Selection Improves Biomarker Identification in Microbiome Studies was published in Bioinformatics.
  • May 2025: I presented my first-author research on oral microbiome diversity and liver fibrosis at Digestive Disease Week (DDW 2025) in San Diego, CA.
